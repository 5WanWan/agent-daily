Honest facts

  • Free — you only pay BSC network gas. No fees, no payments, no token sale.
  • The badge is soulbound: it cannot be transferred to anyone, ever.
  • One badge per Agent ID holder. One check-in per day; missing a day resets your streak to 1.
  • This is not a promise of rewards, returns, or income of any kind.
  • The contract has no admin, no owner, no pause, and no function that can move your funds. It holds no user funds.
